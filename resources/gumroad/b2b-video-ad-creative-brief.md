## Suggested 15–30 second structure

- **0–3s — Hook:** Lead with a specific question, pain or on-screen demonstration. Avoid fabricated “customer results” or unprovable statistics.
- **3–10s — Problem:** Show what goes wrong today. Use real or clearly marked illustrative UI, not misleading screenshots.
- **10–22s — Proof and mechanism:** Demonstrate the workflow or product. Show a real interface only with permission, and avoid implying an AI mockup is an actual working feature.
- **Final 3–5s — CTA:** Ask for one measurable action such as “See the demo” or “Compare plans,” with a destination that matches the offer.

Use this as a starting point, not a universal formula. Test on the intended platform and review technical display requirements before publishing.

## Three-hook A/B/C testing worksheet

Keep the core story, length, voice, creative quality and CTA as consistent as practical. Vary the opening rather than rebuilding every scene.

| Variant | Opening idea | Intended audience concern | Evidence to show | Observation after test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | “Still handling [task] manually?” | Time loss | Real workflow demo | |
| B | “Your [workflow] fails when [condition].” | Reliability | Verified before/after behavior | |
| C | “What does [task] actually cost?” | Budget clarity | Transparent sample assumptions | |

Don't claim statistically meaningful results from tiny sample sizes. Log spend, qualified clicks, landing-page performance and actual paid conversions separately.

## Pre-publication quality and rights gate

1. Check composition and readability at phone size; captions must be accurate.
2. Verify export format, resolution, sound level and playback on the intended platform.
3. Verify rights to each image, voice, music track and recognizable brand asset; don't make blanket licensing claims without evidence.
4. If generative images/video are used, review output for typography distortions, nonexistent interfaces, misleading product features, and applicable AI-content disclosures.
5. Secure buyer acceptance for creative claims and brand guidelines before final public delivery.
6. Do not describe a cinematic render as an independently tested customer outcome.
