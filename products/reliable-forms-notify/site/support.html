Support

For help with rules, quotas, trigger health, licensing, or installation, contact Reliable Forms Notify support.

Support email

support@askouresystems.com

This address must be verified before Marketplace publication.

Before contacting support

  1. Confirm the form response appears in the Form.
  2. Check trigger health in the dashboard.
  3. Check Google daily recipient quota.
  4. Check the monthly app allowance.
  5. Review recent activity/error entries.

What to include

Please include the problem description, approximate time, trigger-health state, and any visible error message. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or unnecessary form-response data.
