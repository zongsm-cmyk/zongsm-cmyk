Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Summary

Reliable Forms Notify is designed to minimize data collection. Notification rules, recent activity logs, and configuration are stored within the Google Apps Script environment associated with the add-on. The add-on does not read your Gmail inbox.

Google data used

Reliable Forms Notify accesses only the Google Form in which it is installed, sends email on the user's behalf, creates and manages its own form-submit trigger, and displays its interface inside Google Forms. These permissions are used only to provide the notification service.

Form response data

When a form is submitted, the add-on temporarily processes submitted answers to evaluate conditions and render the configured email. Core form response content is not intentionally sent to an external analytics or advertising service.

Email data

The add-on sends email through Google Apps Script MailApp. It does not request permission to read the user's Gmail inbox.

Storage

Rules, compact operational status data, recent activity/error entries, monthly usage counters, and plan state are stored in Google Apps Script properties. Reliable Forms Notify does not operate a separate database for form response content in its core notification service.

Payments and licensing

If paid features are enabled, purchase and license information may be processed by the payment provider disclosed on the pricing or purchase page. Payment card information is handled by the payment provider and is not stored by the add-on.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share Google user data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Users can delete notification rules and uninstall the add-on at any time. Operational data stored in Google Apps Script properties remains under the associated Google environment until removed by the add-on, the user, or Google according to platform behavior.

Security

We use least-privilege permissions and intentionally avoid Gmail inbox-read access. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated when product capabilities or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Use the support page for privacy and support requests.
